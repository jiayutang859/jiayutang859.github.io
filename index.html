My Research Vision

Understanding, predicting, and modulating biological interactions in humans.

Not one biological object, but one reusable way of working: a five-stage cycle that can be applied to interactions at any scale, from an antibody meeting its antigen to a diet meeting the gut microbiome.

  1. 01 · Observe

    Observe

    Observe an interaction

  2. 02 · Understand

    Understand

    Understand the objects, context, and environment

  3. 03 · Model & predict

    Model & Predict

    Model the interaction and predict influential factors

  4. 04 · Validate

    Validate

    Validate predicted factors experimentally

  5. 05 · Modulate / Engineer

    Modulate & Engineer

    Improve the interaction by modulating or engineering those factors

Same cycle, different scales.
  • antibody · antigen
  • molecule · cell
  • diet · gut microbiome · host
  • intervention · human physiology

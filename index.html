Who I am

Jiayu Tang (汤家育)

Now
I work on computational antibody engineering with experimental validation. Specifically, I study how the VH–VL framework reshapes de novo antibody design. Currently, I am a Research Assistant in the Wang Lab (Department of Infectious Diseases and Immunology, College of Veterinary Medicine, University of Florida), supervised by Dr. Yiquan Wang.
Path
Before joining UF, I earned my M.S. at UNL under the advisement of Dr. Edward Deehan. Over two years of research there, I coordinated a randomized crossover clinical trial, led a systematic review screened with machine-learning tools, and completed a minor in statistics. Before that, I earned dual bachelor’s degrees (B.Sc. & B.Eng.) through the “3+1” program between the University of Nebraska–Lincoln and Northwest A&F University (China).
Skills
My multidisciplinary experience has trained me in a range of techniques, including computational antibody modeling, multivariate analysis, high-throughput methods, robotic automation, and clinical trial design and coordination.
Research
Current Research Focus

My current research focuses on understanding and predicting how specific antibody contexts (such as sequence and structure) shape better antigen recognition, with the goal of informing antibody design and engineering.

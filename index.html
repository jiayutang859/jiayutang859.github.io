About

Who I Am

Gainesville, FL

I am currently a Research Assistant in the Wang Lab, Department of Infectious Diseases and Immunology at the University of Florida, working with Dr. Yiquan Wang. My work develops computational pipelines — combining AlphaFold 3 structure prediction with generative protein design — to study how antibody framework architecture interacts with epitope geometry.

I also hold an M.Sc. in Food Science and Technology from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, where I worked with Dr. Edward Deehan on a clinical trial and systematic review exploring the effects of dietary pulses on human health and the gut microbiome. I earned dual Bachelor's degrees from Northwest A&F University (NWAFU) and the University of Nebraska–Lincoln (UNL) through a 3+1 dual-degree program.
